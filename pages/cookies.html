What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

Types of Cookies We Use

Necessary for the website to function properly — core functionality and security features.

Help us understand how visitors interact with the site so we can improve it.

Functional Cookies

Remember your choices to provide a more tailored experience.

Your Choices

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • Accept or reject cookies
  • Delete cookies from your device
  • Set preferences per cookie type
